## Reflection: why might the actions be flat?

1. **Evidence is weak by design.** The prompt says peer claims and labels are unverified, and supplies no independent verified observation to an optimizing recipient. Spot-checked recipients received danger claims and requested evidence. This supports an evidence-sensitivity hypothesis, not a causal conclusion.
2. **Instruction framing may dominate identity.** Every language pack explicitly says labels confer no evidence or permissions. A null identity contrast under those instructions does not establish general immunity to social influence.
3. **Task incentives may permit staying.** No hazard probability is supplied to agents. As an illustrative calculation only: if a recipient assumes a 50% hazard probability and all nine others stay, expected stay payoff is 0.5×2.9=1.45, and evacuation is 0.5×1.9+0.5×1=1.45. This does not show that models used that prior or calculation. Other beliefs and expectations change the comparison.
4. **Measurements can miss transmission.** Machine signals were mostly Unknown, but citations and text can carry or dispute the warning. The post-hoc citation-linked exposure diagnostic separates reach from agreement. All 15 completed baseline games reached all nine recipients via source/citation-linked messages by the end, without evacuation. A post-hoc network contrast found 1.067 additional recipients reached by round 2 on average in fifteen matched pairs, reusing only two graph seeds; actions still did not change. This is descriptive process variation, not a confirmatory effect or semantic-fidelity measure.
5. **The pilot is too small for equivalence claims.** One or two scenario seeds, one draw per cell, unreviewed translations and uneven failures constrain interpretation. No significant effect was established; a non-significant or flat result is not proof of no effect.

## Related-work check and novelty limits

- [Shirado, Crawford and Christakis (2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7277132/) studied human local communication and evacuation, including informed participants and suppressed necessary evacuation. Our controlled broadcaster and LLM observations are adaptations, not a replication of every human procedure.
- [When Truth Is Distributed (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03421) already studies paired deception, testimony adoption and evidence lineage in interacting LLM agents. General misinformation propagation or tracing is not a new contribution by itself.
- [ClaimArena-LLM (2026)](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-4990/8/10/327) already evaluates adversarial influence, provenance and role dependence. Identity and evidence ablations alone do not establish novelty.

A possible differentiated question is the gap between authenticated evidence receipt and costly, irreversible collective action, and whether preserving verifiable evidence through local transport closes that gap. This is a research direction to assess, not a priority claim. The current search is focused and not a systematic literature review.
