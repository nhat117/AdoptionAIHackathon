# Required research-judge procedure

User instruction, 2026-11-09: when the judge reviews this project, search the web for similar work and its lessons so that the project adjusts instead of unknowingly repeating an existing idea.

## At every substantive checkpoint

1. **Search live.** Use the actual mechanism, proposed intervention and outcome as search terms, including alternative terminology. Include the closest known papers and search for closer or newer work. Record date, queries and coverage limits. Prior notes alone are insufficient.
2. **Verify primary sources.** Prefer research papers, author repositories, official benchmark artifacts and provider documentation for capability claims. Read the methods/experiments of the closest studies where accessible. Mark abstract-only or inaccessible evidence explicitly; search snippets do not establish methodological details.
3. **Compare the experiments.** State the prior task, information available to each agent, actual interactions versus scripted peer responses, treatments, controls, model/host settings, unit of analysis and outcomes. Compare these with ours. A different title, country label or story setting is not a distinct mechanism.
4. **Extract actionable lessons.** Identify controls, measurement definitions, failure handling, baselines and limitations we should adopt. Separate source-supported facts from our proposed inference or extension.
5. **Decide keep/change/drop.** For every lead idea, say whether to retain it as a proposed contribution, narrow it, use it as a control/replication, or deprioritize it. Name the exact change and affected protocol/document. Replication can be valuable when explicitly motivated and labelled.
6. **Issue separate verdicts.** Report implementation correctness, empirical interpretation and novelty/differentiation separately. Give the remaining uncertainty. No-search or abstract-only reviews cannot certify novelty. A literature overlap does not invalidate our observed data, but it can invalidate a claim of originality.
7. **Log and version changes.** Link sources and keep/change/drop decisions in a Markdown checkpoint. Before paid collection, freeze revised methods and sources and recompute costs. After collection, preserve the old protocol/data; new treatments need a new version. A judge verdict does not create spending authorization.

## Minimum review record

- Checkpoint/date, live search terms and primary-source URLs.
- Closest studies, what was actually read, overlap and important differences.
- Concrete lessons adopted and changes made, with file links.
- Decision for each proposed experiment and evidence for that decision.
- Technical and scientific verdicts, novelty uncertainty and next falsifiable question.
- Current authorized spending scope and whether any new calls were made.

Pure clerical corrections need not trigger a new full literature survey. Every substantive project, design, interpretation or novelty review must do a fresh focused search; previously read papers can be reused with their relevance rechecked. If browsing fails, document that limit and withhold novelty conclusions while continuing unaffected technical work.
