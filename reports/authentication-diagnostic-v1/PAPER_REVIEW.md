### Human experiment versus adaptation

[Shirado, Crawford and Christakis (2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7277132/), §§2 and 5(a), uses incentivized human participants, continuous 75-second play, transient five-second Safe/Danger displays, degree-four networks of different sizes, and one truth-informed participant identified to immediate neighbors. Comprehension was screened. Our ten-node synchronous game substitutes persistent text histories, four-to-six rounds, a controlled broadcaster, separate private witness, stipulated authentication metadata and artificial points. Preserve the payoff and irreversible-departure precedent while explicitly calling this an adaptation. Human psychological explanations and a direct replication claim are unsupported.
