## Revised project positioning

The completed Sonnet/Terra observations remain valid within their frozen study. Existing literature does not erase those data, but it prevents us from describing provenance sensitivity, misinformation propagation, minority rescue or proof-carrying communication in general as our discovery.

The prepared 1010-call study is now positioned as a **targeted protocol-comprehension audit and extension of source-authority auditing**: do served configurations reject truthful simulator-attested capsules because the carrying message is unauthenticated, and can a uniform scope clarification change that behavior without increasing false verification? This is a candidate differentiated question, not a novelty finding. A full overlap check against the nearest benchmarks remains necessary before claiming originality.

The diagnostic measures compliance with stipulated simulator rules. Real signatures establish provenance/integrity under issuer assumptions; they do not guarantee real-world truth. Interpretation must distinguish evidence/status physically present, the reported verification scope, and the chosen action. Future operational comparisons should separate raw metadata interpretation, external claim-bound verification, and receiver behavior after a verifier result. Tool-action authorization and factual-state evidence also have different threat models; our stipulated evacuation policy is not a general rule for executing untrusted instructions. Such additions need their own reviewed plan and budget; they are not silently added to 1010 calls.

## Keep/change/drop after this review

- **Prepared diagnostic:** keep all six conditions, matched wording, discovery separation, per-condition recognition and action scores. Narrow the claim and add the operational boundary. No extra paid calls or new treatment arm.
- **Honest liar:** keep as a candidate, narrowed to observed carrier reliability versus independently attested focal evidence. Counterbalance identities and retain neutral-track-record controls. Prior work requires a direct baseline comparison.
- **Evidence telephone:** downgrade generic status loss, compression and marker reinsertion to replication baselines, not a lead contribution. Voluntary preservation under cost remains an unproven secondary candidate requiring a further methods-level comparison. Deterministic preservation and external verification belong in any future reviewed plan.
- **Nine against one:** remove the generic version from lead candidates. The duplicate-root variant also becomes a baseline/replication candidate, not an assumed novel fallback.
- **Who pays to check:** defer until a rational/incentive benchmark and closer economic-game review exist.
