## What the actual Word paper establishes, if its reported analysis checks out

Its strongest contribution is a concrete language-by-wording reversal in a fixed budget-reporting task. The reported4,320-answer follow-up has20task clusters, three languages and three wordings. In no-record attack cells, the largest false-target rate belongs to Chinese, English and Vietnamese under wordings1,2,3respectively. The table arithmetic supports the stated Chinese-minus-English change:48.75points under wording1 to−20points under wording2, a−68.75-point interaction. Its reported task-block interval and Holm-adjustedp=.000488 are much stronger evidence for this narrow interaction than the evacuation diagnostic's nonsignificant wording contrast. A smallp-value does not establish originality or a general language effect.

The180case pilot reports179admissible outputs,71wrongreports,7unsupportedrecordattributions and2wrongreportsallowed. Those two cases expose a specific missing check: the gate trusts the worker's “record” label plus availability of a current record, but never binds the chosen amount to that record. This is an operationally meaningful counterexample to the tested weak gate, not a novel robust provenance mechanism. The72/72wrong-current-record result deliberately corrupts the trusted input assumption; it does not demonstrate a real record-system compromise or that no stronger independent check could work. The Word paper now states these limits honestly.

The controller, scripted peer messages, one GPT-5 worker and downstream rule/monitor form a simulated workflow. That can be relevant multi-component safety research, but it is not equivalent to a population of live agents repeatedly changing each other's histories and actions. The evacuation main study actually has ten communicating agents, persistent local histories, multiple rounds and irreversible departures. Its frozen-view diagnostic is separately single-turn; that part cannot be counted as live swarm evidence.
