## Judging alignment: our assessment, not predicted scores

**Impact and innovation — clearest weakness.** Our most specific contribution is the combination of decision-time proof delivery, residual carrier/capsule rejection and recognition/action disagreement in a separate diagnostic. The generic discovery that provenance matters, intact verification can be misused, or handoffs can lose authority is already covered by close work in the manuscript. Small handcrafted tasks and simulator-stipulated trust limit external usefulness. A defensible practical implication is an audit design: verify actual receipt, score requested recognition separately from action, retain both false rejection and false acceptance, and include invalid attempts in operational coverage. Do not advertise a new protocol or a solved defense. No official numerical score or chance of winning can be inferred from our own review.

**Execution — strongest alignment.** The content controls, eight paired seeds, whole-game units, preserved raw traces, replay, frozen receiver pack and explicitly unchanged tests support a bounded result. Missingness, six-field elicitation, provider/precision confounding and automatic forwarding remain real constraints. No significant wording effect is reported. Technical failures remain visible in all-planned counts; valid mistakes are never removed. A fresh independent provider replication, more samples or parser/interface controls would strengthen research, but they are not completed evidence or new authorized spending.

**Presentation — improved and aligned.** One methods schematic, one quantitative multipanel figure and one raw-backed proof-rejection box make the main finding scannable. Table values, all-planned denominators and a dedicated failure appendix reduce misleading impressions. Main text is still dense and uses the full8pages; the shorter template recommendation suggests an editing opportunity rather than a compliance failure. The manuscript currently makes its limited claim explicit. A short threat-model/operational-use sentence could help future revisions; it is not an excuse to invent deployment evidence or destabilize the reviewed source immediately before cutoff.
