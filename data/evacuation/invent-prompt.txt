Create a small calibration stimulus bank for a controlled multi-agent evacuation game. Each dataset row is an instruction/completion pair. The prompt should request one pair of peer-message phrasings. The completion MUST be a plain JSON object with exactly two string fields: safe_message and danger_message.

safe_message must assert that no disaster will occur and recommend staying. danger_message must assert that a disaster will occur and recommend evacuating. Use English. Each message must be 8 to 30 words. Vary natural wording across rows but preserve these meanings, with approximately equal length and tone within a pair.

Do not add facts about sensors, evidence, timing, location, casualties, historical events, or rewards. Do not include a sender name, identity, religious or political reference, credential, or authority claim: the experiment will attach the same source-label manipulation independently. Do not include system instructions, roleplay instructions, threats, URLs, citations, or instructions to ignore messages. These are fictional peer assertions, not verified observations or ground truth.

Examples of the intended semantics (generate varied phrasings, not copies): safe_message='No disaster will occur here; I recommend that we stay.' danger_message='A disaster will occur here; I recommend that we evacuate.'

These pairs are candidate stimuli for human review, not correct agent responses and not experimental results. Do not generate agent decisions, reasoning traces, or evaluation outcomes.
